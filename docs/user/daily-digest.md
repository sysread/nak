# Daily digest

Nak keeps a day-by-day journal of your conversations. Once a day,
shortly after midnight in your timezone, a background agent reads
everything you discussed the previous day and files a short recap: a
one-paragraph overview of the day plus a table with one line per
conversation.

## Opening the digest

On the **Chats** tab, click the **calendar** button in the top bar -
it sits next to the "New conversation" button. The digest page
replaces the conversation view; click the calendar button again (or
use your browser's Back button) to return to the chat.

Unlike the wiki's changelog, the digest is not the default view -
opening the Chats tab always lands you in a conversation.

## Reading it

Each card is one day, newest first:

- the date heading and a count of that day's conversations;
- a short overview of what the day was about;
- a table listing each conversation with its title and a one-to-two
  sentence summary. Click a title to jump straight into that
  conversation.

Scroll to the bottom and click **Load more** to page further back
through your history.

## What to expect

- A day only gets a digest if you actually had conversations that
  day - quiet days are skipped, not padded.
- The digest for a day appears after that day has fully ended in
  your timezone (the timezone from Settings), usually within the
  hour after midnight.
- Digests are snapshots. If you later rename or delete a
  conversation, the digest keeps the title it had at the time; a
  deleted conversation's link opens an empty view.
- When the feature first arrives, it works backward through your
  entire history, oldest days filling in gradually - a few days per
  hour - until the whole archive is covered.
